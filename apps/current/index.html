A sound. A little space.

Current makes simple tones in your browser. You choose the pitch, the beat, and the space around them.

Three ways to listen

Binaural: a different frequency in each ear. Headphones keep the tones separate; the difference can be perceived as a beat.

Isochronic: one tone pulsed at your chosen rate, with smooth edges. Works with headphones or speakers.

Monaural: two tones mixed into both channels, creating an acoustic beat. Works with headphones or speakers.

About the evidence

Research on binaural beats and brain activity is inconsistent. A 2023 systematic review found that the available studies did not give an unequivocal answer about brainwave entrainment. A steady tone can still be part of a personal listening ritual; an effect on your EEG, focus or health is not guaranteed.

Private, and yours

No account, cookies, analytics, or audio uploads. Presets stay in your browser. A shared URL includes the sound settings, not your listening history. All tones and ambient layers are generated locally. The downloaded HTML includes everything needed to play offline.

Golden Lift

Starts with 220 Hz in the left ear and 230 Hz in the right, with a quiet 277 + 330 Hz chord. Its pulse moves from 80 BPM to 115 BPM, then returns to 80 during the fade. Centre the beat for a 215 / 225 Hz pair.
